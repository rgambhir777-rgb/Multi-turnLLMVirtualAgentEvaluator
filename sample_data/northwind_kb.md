## Plans

### Basic 100 plan
The Basic 100 internet plan provides 100 Mbps download speed and costs $39.99 per month.

### Fiber 500 plan
The Fiber 500 internet plan provides 500 Mbps download speed and costs $59.99 per month.

### Fiber Gig plan
The Fiber Gig internet plan provides 1 Gbps (1000 Mbps) download speed and costs $79.99 per month.

### Contract term
All internet plans have a 12-month contract term. Plans include unlimited data with no data caps.

## Plan changes

### Upgrades
Upgrading to a faster plan is free of charge. The new plan takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

### Downgrades
Downgrading to a slower plan carries a $10 plan change fee. The new plan takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

## Billing

### Billing cycle
Bills are issued on the 1st of each month and payment is due on the 21st of the month.

### Late payment fee
A $8 late payment fee is charged when a payment is more than 10 days overdue.

### Autopay discount
Customers enrolled in autopay receive a $5 per month discount.

### Router rental
Router rental costs $10 per month and appears as a separate line on the bill.

### Billing disputes
Billing disputes are investigated within 10 business days. The disputed amount is not subject to late fees while the investigation is open.

## Refunds

### Refund processing time
Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days back to the original payment method.

### Refund request window
Refund requests must be made within 30 days of the charge.

## Outages

### Outage status check
Customers can check outage status at northwind.example/status or by texting STATUS to 55123.

### Outage procedure
For an outage report the agent verifies the service address, checks for a known outage in the area, and if none is found runs a remote line test before scheduling a technician.

### Technician visits
Technician visits are free when the fault is on the Northwind network.

### Outage credit
Customers receive a $5 service credit for each full day of an outage lasting longer than 24 hours. The credit is applied on the next bill.

## Account security

### Password reset
Customers can reset their password at northwind.example/reset. A one-time code is sent by SMS to the phone number on file and is valid for 10 minutes.

### Account lockout
After 5 failed sign-in attempts the online account is locked for 30 minutes.

### Password handling
Northwind agents never ask customers for their full password.

### Account verification
Accounts are verified with the account number (format NW- followed by 6 digits) plus the ZIP code on file.

## Cancellation

### Early termination fee
Cancelling within the 12-month contract term incurs a $150 early termination fee. There is no fee after the contract term ends.

### Cancellation timing
Service stays active until the end of the current billing cycle after a cancellation request.

### Equipment return
Rented equipment must be returned within 30 days of cancellation, otherwise a $120 non-return fee applies.

## Support

### Phone support hours
Phone support is available from 8 AM to 10 PM Eastern Time, 7 days a week.

### Chat and email support
Live chat support is available 24/7. Email support responds within 24 hours.

### Scope of support
Northwind virtual agents help with Northwind Telecom accounts, billing, plans, outages and technical support only.
